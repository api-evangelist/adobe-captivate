Follow a parcel from preparation to final delivery in this accessible, scenario-based learning experience. Combining practical decisions, varied interactions and a friendly 3D visual style, the project showcases accessibility features including structured reading and tab orders, captions, and timed or untimed activities. Explore a final quiz, a downloadable recap and a reusable approach to accessible learning that can be adapted to onboarding, operational procedures or process training.
