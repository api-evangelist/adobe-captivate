We’re excited to share Adobe Captivate 13.2, our latest minor release. While this isn’t a major feature release, it brings together a set of meaningful enhancements that make everyday authoring smoother; from a better Avatar (AI Narrator) experience and stronger accessibility to smoother project upgrades and important quality and stability fixes. We’ve been listening closely to how authors use Captivate every day, what saves time, what creates friction, and where seemingly small improvements can make a real difference.
